From quote to payment

Five steps, and your customer is served.

  1. 01

    Create the quote

    Customer, items: the total, VAT, deposit and balance due are calculated for you. The customer name is optional.

  2. 02

    Speak instead of typing

    Say "three bags of cement at five thousand": the item is added. Your saved products are one tap away in the catalog.

  3. 03

    Get it signed on the screen

    Hand over the phone: your customer signs, and their name appears under the signature. The quote becomes an invoice in one tap.

  4. 04

    Send it on WhatsApp

    The PDF goes out while your customer is still interested. You can also print it, or issue a thermal receipt.

  5. 05

    Track and follow up

    Today’s and this month’s sales, what is still to collect. An unsettled invoice? A polite reminder goes out on WhatsApp in one tap.

Frequently asked questions

What people often ask us.

Does DevisPro work without internet?

Yes. You create, edit and save quotes and invoices offline. Internet is only used to send a document on WhatsApp or by email, and for voice input depending on your phone settings.

Where are my invoices stored?

On your phone only. DevisPro does not send your data to any server. Make a backup from the settings from time to time.

Which currencies are available?

CFA franc (FCFA, XOF, XAF), Guinean franc, Congolese franc, naira, cedi, dirham, dinar, euro, dollar and more: 16 currencies in total.

Can I print?

Yes, in A4 on any printer connected to the phone, or as a 58 mm and 80 mm thermal receipt.

How do I check that an invoice is authentic?

Open the scanner from the dashboard and point at the document’s QR code, or pick a photo. DevisPro tells you whether the invoice exists in your records and whether the amount matches.

How do I get a license?

Some advanced features require a license. Write to us on WhatsApp from the app or this website, we will help you.
