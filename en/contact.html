Frequently asked questions

What people often ask us.

Does DevisPro work without internet?

Yes. You create, edit and save quotes and invoices offline. Internet is only used to send a document on WhatsApp or by email, and for voice input depending on your phone settings.

Where are my invoices stored?

On your phone only. DevisPro does not send your data to any server. Make a backup from the settings from time to time.

Which currencies are available?

CFA franc (FCFA, XOF, XAF), Guinean franc, Congolese franc, naira, cedi, dirham, dinar, euro, dollar and more: 16 currencies in total.

Can I print?

Yes, in A4 on any printer connected to the phone, or as a 58 mm and 80 mm thermal receipt.

How do I check that an invoice is authentic?

Open the scanner from the dashboard and point at the document’s QR code, or pick a photo. DevisPro tells you whether the invoice exists in your records and whether the amount matches.

How do I get a license?

Choose a duration in the Pricing section, then send the WhatsApp message that opens. You receive a code to paste into the app. Nothing renews automatically.
