Privacy policy

Your data belongs to you. No cloud, no trackers.

01Introduction

This policy describes how the DevisPro application handles and protects its users’ information. We take privacy seriously.

02About the application

DevisPro is a quote and invoice application for businesses. It creates documents, exports them as PDF and tracks sales. Everything happens on the phone.

03Data collected

The application does not collect any personal data on external servers. What you enter (business name, tax number, logo, signature, customers) stays on your device.

An anonymous device identifier is used only for the technical management of your licence.

04No online storage

No data is sent to a server and no online database is used. You keep full control of your files.

05Your rights

You can edit or delete your data at any time. Uninstalling the application permanently deletes all data stored on the phone.

06Product catalogue

The products, services and prices in your catalogue are strictly personal and stored on your phone.

07Export and sharing

Excel export creates a file on your device so you can share it. PDFs are sent only through the applications you choose (WhatsApp, e-mail…), under your responsibility.

08Third-party services

The application uses software libraries to produce PDFs and charts and to read QR codes. They run on the phone and transmit none of your information.

09Permissions

Microphone: for voice entry. Speech is converted to text by your phone’s speech recognition service, on the device or online depending on your settings. DevisPro does not record or keep any audio.

Gallery: to add your logo or stamp.

Camera: to scan an invoice QR code and check that it exists in your DevisPro. The image is read on the device, offline; nothing is saved or sent.

10Security

The security of your data depends first on your phone’s. We recommend protecting it with a code or fingerprint.

11Your responsibility

You alone are responsible for backing up your data. Losing, theft or resetting the phone without a backup means the data is lost for good. Back up regularly from the settings.

12Ads and trackers

DevisPro contains no advertising and uses no analytics or tracking tools.

13Changes

This policy may be updated. The latest version is always available on this page.

14Consent

By using the application, you accept that your data is stored on your phone and acknowledge that deleting the application deletes that data.
